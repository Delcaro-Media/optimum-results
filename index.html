FIELD SURVEYS DONE PROPERLY

Not all LDAR surveys are created equal. We evaluate every survey against six critical criteria to ensure defensible, regulator-ready results.

  • Correct detection technologies — selected for the components and site conditions encountered
  • Qualified field personnel — trained, competent, and experienced with each detection method deployed. Technicians have operational experience and background.
  • Complete component coverage — all applicable areas are surveyed
  • Proper leak definitions applied — the regulatory leak definition is applied and adhered to
  • Accurate documentation — recorded in the client's database or a custom database we provide — so the data stands up to audit scrutiny
  • Timely, transparent reporting — any hazardous leaks are reported immediately to operations. Survey results are reviewed with operations upon completion.

Surveys are completed with all information being shared with operations immediately for discussion and clarification.

FIELD-DRIVEN PROGRAM DESIGN

An effective LDAR program starts with operations — built on four pillars:

✓ Regulatory compliance
✓ Operational input and support
✓ Maintenance scheduling and execution
✓ Effective oversight protocol
  • Regulation-aligned survey schedules — built around AER, ECCC, BC OGC, or SK requirements
  • Technology selection matrices — matching detection methods to site-specific conditions
  • Leak repair tracking & escalation workflows — so nothing slips through the cracks
  • Constant awareness of changing regulations — ensuring your program always reflects current requirements
  • Operator training outlines — so operations can support the LDAR program
  • Any leak presenting a hazard is immediately brought to operations' attention

We work directly with operations, EHS, and management stakeholders to build programs and conduct surveys that are practical, sustainable, and defensible — not shelf-ware that satisfies paperwork but fails in the field.
